Meet BlinkSnap, a hands-free camera concept for Raven Prism. Stay in the moment, and capture it with two blinks. In this simulator, pressing B creates a short tracking loss. A second blink saves the illustrated scene locally. Pause whenever you need a break. The detector checks timing, tracking recovery, and a cooldown to reduce accidental captures. Ready for another memory? Blink twice again. Choose Use camera to capture from a real camera. This demo uses simulated input. The tracking-loss heuristic still needs testing on Raven hardware. BlinkSnap. Two blinks. One memory.
